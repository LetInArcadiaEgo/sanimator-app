      How it works
      • Hold a face and speak to add a spoken message. Tap a face to type one.
      • To make one character cut in on the other, press the second face while you are still holding the first.
      • Tap a message to fix its words.
      • Press and hold a message for its options. Keep holding and drag to move it.
      • On Preview, while paused, tap a portrait to pick a photo and tap a name to change the name and colour.
      Paste a script

      One message per line, starting with A: or B: or a character's name.
